Privacy policy

Juvena Gmail reader · Last updated 2026-10-08

Who uses this app

This is a personal, single-user app. It connects only to the Gmail account of its owner, who also operates it. No other people's accounts are accessed.

What data it accesses

  • Read: email messages and threads (headers, text body, attachment names), to find and summarize mail the owner cares about.
  • Compose: creating, editing, and deleting draft messages that the app itself created. The app never sends email. The owner reviews and sends every draft himself in Gmail.

How data is used and stored

  • Email content is used only to provide features to the owner: summaries, alerts about important messages, and draft replies.
  • The OAuth token is stored on the owner's private server, readable only by the service that calls the Gmail API.
  • To produce summaries and drafts, relevant email content is processed by an AI model provider acting on the owner's behalf. It is not used to build advertising profiles or sold to anyone.
  • No data is shared with third parties for any other purpose.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Juvena's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
